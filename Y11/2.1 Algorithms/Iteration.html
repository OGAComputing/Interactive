Write an algorithm that switches on the air conditioning once it gets too warm.

Scenario

Ronald wants to switch on his air conditioning automatically once the room gets too warm. His program should keep asking for the current temperature until it is above 30 degrees, at which point it switches the air conditioning on.

Your algorithm must:

  • Ask for the current temperature
  • If the temperature is over 30, output "switch on air conditioning"
  • Keep repeating the question until the air conditioning is switched on

Extra challenge (not marked): once switched on, keep checking the temperature until it drops below 15, then output "Switch off air conditioning".
